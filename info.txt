Faction Description:

The drifters, a faction formed from a creed of brotherhood that lasted as the dominating force for many years bringing order and strength to Chernarus (S1-2). 

After years of fighting the growing bandit threat, the drifters disbanded, leaving Chernarus to protect its people in the wake of a bandit take over of the land. The remaining drifters went into hiding living amongst the new world and observing its trials and errors (S3).

Eventually they arose in silence as the Forgotten Few until their new recruits were ready to accept the drifters past, a past discovered in a hidden weapons stash in Novoselki (S4). 

The newly reformed drifters returned to build a new image and a new home amongst friends and family alike in Chernogorsk City (S5). 

After years of financial and political success a cold front swept the south. Due to the Southern Empires position Drifters leadership were able to safely enact an emergency evacuation filling their trucks and vehicles with food, water, medical and military supplies for the trip to their new home…Livonia, where we dominated the East making ourselves the only faction to reside In or around Topolin City and securing more land and wealth than anyone could have expected. (S6-S7)

After the Empire sent a colonizing group to Chernarus after the remnants of the the cold front had passed, The Drifter Empire became under siege and after 7 long nights of explosions, gun shots, flies and death the Drifter Military had finally lost their home in NovoD City (S8)
=
The remaining civilians and soldiers after the war drifted west to the city of Novaya Petrovka. In honor of the empire they didn’t know if they’d see again they created the Drifter Creed  a religion they could support and build upon. They learned from their mistakes and built beautiful castle walls from the remaining ruins around Chernarus that surrounded the city. They built a radio tower to communicate again with the open world and training grounds to prepare should an enemy ever threaten the family again. After losing connection to the empire in Livonia the Drifters renamed to the commonwealth and went on to live and fight again. (TWD S2)

After years of peace and growth in Novaya Petrovka, groups grew jealous of the success and strength brought on by the Drifter Organization. After countless victories defending the city, destabilizing many of The Commonwealths enemies, and building a strong alliance for the CW; the Drifter Organization chose to leave Novaya Petrovka in hopes of starting a new journey with their newfound brothers and continuing the creed they started long ago. Now the Drifter Empire is back and ready to claim yet another page in history in Zelenogorsk City (RP OFFICIAL S1)
